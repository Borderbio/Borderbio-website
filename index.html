Evidence-based Agroecology.

Science from the soil up.

We investigate how agricultural amendments, including the compounds plants naturally produce, shape the biology in the soil and whether we can put those effects to work in the field.

Putting soil biology to work

Farmers want to see their soil working for them.

Soil microorganisms contribute to processes that underpin productive soils, including nitrogen fixation, phosphorus availability, and carbon storage.

There are many ways to influence soil biology — from traditional farming practices to new technologies such as biological products. The important question is whether they actually change how the soil functions.
